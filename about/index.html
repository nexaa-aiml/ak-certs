>_ ABOUT_NEXAA

NEXAA is the flagship student association dedicated to nurturing innovation in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Automation, and Modern Software Engineering. Through hackathons, algorithmic wars, and robotics sprints, NEXAA empowers engineers to build transformative solutions for real-world impact.

>_ ABOUT_COLLEGE

R.V.R. & J.C. College of Engineering (Autonomous) is an premier technical institution established in 1985 in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. Accredited by NBA and NAAC with an 'A+' grade, the college stands as a center of academic excellence and industry-aligned innovation.
